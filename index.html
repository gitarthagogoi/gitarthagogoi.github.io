About

I'm an embedded engineer based in Ahmedabad. At work I do both sides of the board: firmware in C on ARM Cortex-M and ESP32, and the hardware it runs on. Most of my time goes into sensor and actuator interfacing, communication between controllers, and control loops that have to keep running unattended.

I started with robotics in school, competing at the World Robot Olympiad nationals in 2016 and 2017, and kept building things through a BTech in electronics and an MTech in digital communication and signal processing. The MTech produced two journal papers on intelligent reflecting surfaces for wireless systems. That research is simulation work, but it left me comfortable with the maths behind filtering, estimation and optimisation, which is useful when a control loop or a noisy sensor misbehaves.

The problems I enjoy most sit where hardware and firmware meet: a bus that works in loopback and nowhere else, an error counter that climbs for no obvious reason, a peripheral that is configured correctly and still silent. Right now I'm going deeper into the layer below the application: startup code, linker scripts, bootloaders, and secure boot.

Projects

Hybrid IRS for MU-MISO: Energy and Spectral Efficiency

Academic research (MTech) · Published 2026

A hybrid intelligent reflecting surface mixes powered (active) and unpowered (passive) elements. Active elements beat the double path loss of a passive surface but cost power, so the question is how many to switch on. This work studies decode-and-forward aided hybrid IRS precoding for a multi-user MISO downlink with power-aware allocation of active and passive elements.

The public repository holds the system model and the benchmark schemes the paper compares against: no IRS, random-phase, optimised passive and fully active. The proposed hybrid precoder is released as an interface stub only and is available on request for academic use.

MATLAB · CVX · WMMSE · alternating optimisation

Paper (Physical Communication, Elsevier) · Repository

Active IRS-Aided MU-MISO System

Academic research (MTech) · Published 2025

MATLAB implementation of joint base-station beamforming and IRS precoding for an active IRS in a multi-user MISO downlink. The sum-rate problem is solved with fractional programming, alternating optimisation and WMMSE, under base-station and IRS power constraints.

The model includes amplifier hardware power and quantised phase shifts, and compares passive, ideal active and practical active surfaces under weak and strong line-of-sight channels.

MATLAB · fractional programming · WMMSE · QCQP

Paper (Int. J. Information Technology, Springer) · Repository

Hybrid IRS Simulation Framework

Academic research (MTech) · Work in progress

An early-stage modular MATLAB testbed for hybrid (active plus passive) IRS in MU-MISO systems: channel modelling for the three links, a hybrid element structure with gain and phase, a total power model covering base station and IRS hardware, and spectral and energy efficiency evaluation.

The core pipeline runs. The joint optimisation of the base-station precoder and the hybrid IRS matrix, and the documentation, are not finished.

CAN Bus Embedded Guide

Personal · In progress

A CAN bus that does not work usually looks like a firmware bug and is often not one. This is my written-up debugging order for CAN on STM32 and ESP32: physical layer first, then bit timing, then mode, filters and interrupts.

It includes a case study from a bus that ran fine in loopback but never got an ACK in normal mode. After a long pass through bit timing and filter settings, the cause turned out to be the transceiver's RS (slope control) pin, which was wired so the part never entered high-speed mode.

Published so far: CAN basics, bit timing and sample point, and the error checklist. STM32 and ESP32 (TWAI) example code is still to come.

STM32 HAL (bxCAN) · ESP32 TWAI · MCP2551 · SN65HVD230

Smart Energy Meter

Academic (BTech) · Completed 2023

An energy monitor built on a PZEM sensing module with ESP32 nodes. Readings are oversampled and smoothed with a moving-average filter to cut measurement noise, and nodes report over LoRa or GSM so the system does not depend on local Wi-Fi. Presented at ICIMSTAS 2023 as "Smart Energy Meter for Accurate Real-Time Monitoring".
